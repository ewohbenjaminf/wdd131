Discover Nigeria

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Discover Nigeria

The name Discover Nigeria was selected because the website introduces visitors to the country's diverse culture, traditional foods, interesting places, and heritage. The name is simple, memorable, and communicates the purpose of the website clearly.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Discover Nigeria is to provide visitors with useful and engaging information about Nigeria's culture, food, places, traditions, and people. The website will help visitors learn about Nigeria through organized information, photographs, descriptions, and cultural highlights.

The website will contain information about Nigerian foods, notable places to visit, cultural traditions, and additional references for visitors who want to learn more.

Scenarios

Scenario 1

What traditional Nigerian foods should I learn about or try?

A visitor can use the Food page to discover popular Nigerian dishes, their descriptions, and information about their cultural importance.

Scenario 2

What interesting places can I learn about when planning a visit to Nigeria?

A visitor can use the Places page to explore notable locations and learn why they are significant or interesting.

Scenario 3

What Nigerian traditions and cultural practices should I know about?

A visitor can use the Culture page to learn about Nigerian traditions, celebrations, clothing, music, language, and other cultural practices.

Color Scheme

The color scheme is inspired by Nigeria and is designed to create a welcoming, energetic, and easy-to-read experience.

Green

#008751

Used for the header, navigation accents, headings, and important visual elements.

Gold

#D4A017

Used for accents, highlights, borders, and selected interactive elements.

Charcoal

#222222

Used for primary body text and strong contrast.

White

#FFFFFF

Used for backgrounds, cards, and text placed on dark backgrounds.

Typography

Poppins

Use: Page titles, section headings, navigation, and other important headings.

Open Sans

Use: Body paragraphs, descriptions, labels, and supporting information.
